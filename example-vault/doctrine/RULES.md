# Starter doctrine

Keep standing rules short. Put detailed lessons in retrievable records.

1. Screen a candidate before storing it.
2. Record a stable source label and the intended audience.
3. Keep one durable lesson or decision per record when practical.
4. Preserve corrections through explicit supersession; do not silently rewrite history.
5. Recall relevant records before making a claim about a changing system.
6. Treat an explicit zero-result response differently from a failed search.
7. Keep private material out of public examples and release artifacts.
8. Do not store credentials, private keys, access tokens, or personal contact data.

These rules are a small example, not a complete security policy. Adapt them to the consequence of your work and retain the evidence behind each local rule.
